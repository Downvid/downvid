# DownVid Brand and Trademark Usage Policy

**Status:** Project-maintainer guidance  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16

This document explains how to refer to DownVid and its branding without misleading users about who develops or operates a product or service. It is not legal advice and does not claim that any mark is registered.

## 1. Software license and branding are different

DownVid's source code is distributed under the [MIT License](./LICENSE). The MIT License permits use, modification, redistribution, and sale of the software, subject to its terms.

This policy is about the use of the **DownVid name, logo, app icon, and other brand identifiers** to identify a source or to market a product or service. It does not revoke or rewrite rights already granted by the MIT License or any separate asset license. If an asset's actual license or ownership is unclear, that must be verified before making a stronger claim about it.

## 2. Acceptable referential use

You may refer to DownVid accurately, for example:

- In reviews, tutorials, comparisons, documentation, bug reports, and discussions about the project.
- To explain that a fork or integration is based on, compatible with, or uses DownVid, provided the statement is true and does not imply endorsement.
- In installation instructions or screenshots that identify the original project, with a link to the [official repository](https://github.com/Downvid/downvid).

Use only as much of the name or visual identity as reasonably needed to identify the project. Do not edit the logo in a way that suggests the project approved the edit.

## 3. Third-party forks and hosted services

The MIT License does not prohibit commercial use merely because a service charges money. A third party may offer hosting or managed deployment of open-source software, subject to applicable law and the relevant licenses.

However, third-party products and services must not mislead users into believing that they are operated, maintained, sponsored, or officially endorsed by the DownVid maintainers.

For a third-party hosted service or redistributed modified build, the maintainers ask operators to:

- Use a distinct service/product name and, where practical, their own logo and visual identity instead of presenting the service as the official DownVid product.
- Clearly identify the actual operator and provide a visible statement such as: **“Unofficial third-party service. Not operated by or affiliated with the DownVid maintainers.”**
- Use the DownVid name only to make accurate, limited statements about the upstream software, and link to the official repository where appropriate.
- Preserve copyright and license notices required by the applicable software license.

Do not, without explicit permission from the relevant rights holder, use DownVid branding in a way that falsely suggests official status or endorsement—for example, naming a hosted service “DownVid Official Cloud,” using the logo as the dominant identity of a paid service, or using a confusingly similar domain, account, or product name.

## 4. Logo and asset status

The repository contains visual assets such as `app/frontend/src/assets/logo.png` and `app/build/appicon.png`. Their provenance and asset-specific licensing should be documented separately where necessary.

This policy does **not** assume that all logo files are automatically excluded from the MIT License, and it does not assert registered-trademark status or exclusive ownership without verification. Before adding an asset-specific restriction or claiming ownership, the maintainers should confirm the source, contributors, and existing grants, then document any applicable exception or separate license clearly.

## 5. Questions and suspected impersonation

If you are unsure whether a particular use is misleading, link to the official repository and clearly disclose who operates the product. To report a potential impersonation or confusing use, open an issue in the [DownVid repository](https://github.com/Downvid/downvid/issues) and include the public URL and a concise explanation. Do not post private user data.

# DownVid 品牌与商标使用政策

**状态：** 项目维护者指引  
**最后更新：** 2026-10-16

本文用于说明如何准确使用 DownVid 名称与品牌素材，避免用户混淆软件来源、服务运营者或官方背书。本文件不是法律意见，也不代表相关标识已经注册为商标。

## 1. 软件许可与品牌使用是两回事

DownVid 源代码采用 [MIT License](./LICENSE) 发布。MIT 许可允许在遵守其条款的前提下使用、修改、再分发及销售软件。

本政策关注的是 **DownVid 名称、Logo、应用图标及其他品牌标识** 如何用于识别项目，或用于宣传产品与服务。本政策不追溯撤销或改写 MIT 许可及其他素材许可已经授予的权利。如果某个素材的来源或实际授权范围不明确，应先核实，不能未经确认就作出更强的权利声明。

## 2. 允许的指代性使用

在准确、不造成误导的前提下，可以在以下场景中提及 DownVid：

- 评测、教程、比较、文档、问题报告及项目讨论。
- 如实说明某个 fork、集成或部署方案基于 DownVid、兼容 DownVid 或使用了 DownVid，但不得暗示获得官方认可。
- 在安装指南或截图中识别原项目，并适当链接到 [官方仓库](https://github.com/Downvid/downvid)。

只使用识别项目所合理需要的名称或视觉素材。不要以容易让人误认为获得官方批准的方式修改 Logo。

## 3. 第三方分支与托管服务

MIT 许可不会仅仅因为某项服务收费就禁止其商业使用。第三方可以在遵守适用法律及相关许可证的前提下，提供托管或代部署服务。

但第三方产品或服务不得误导用户，使其以为服务由 DownVid 维护者运营、维护、赞助或正式背书。

对于第三方托管服务或重新分发的修改版本，维护者建议运营方：

- 使用能够区分身份的服务/产品名称，并在可行时使用自己的 Logo 和视觉识别，不要把第三方服务包装成官方 DownVid 产品。
- 清楚标明实际运营方，并醒目展示类似声明：**“非官方第三方服务，不由 DownVid 维护者运营，也与其无关联。”**
- 仅在准确说明上游软件时有限度地使用 DownVid 名称，并在适当位置链接官方仓库。
- 保留适用软件许可证所要求的版权与许可声明。

未经相关权利人明确许可，不要以虚假暗示官方身份或背书的方式使用 DownVid 品牌。例如，将托管服务命名为“DownVid 官方云”、把 Logo 作为收费服务的主要品牌，或使用容易造成混淆的域名、账号名或产品名称。

社区部署指南可以准确提及 DownVid 并链接官方仓库。如果第三方为其他用户运营服务，应明确说明运营者是谁，以及该服务是否独立于项目。除非维护者明确指定为官方镜像或服务，否则不要将第三方镜像或服务描述为“DownVid 官方”产品。

## 4. Logo 与素材状态

仓库中包含 `app/frontend/src/assets/logo.png` 与 `app/build/appicon.png` 等视觉素材。必要时应单独记录这些素材的来源及针对素材本身的授权。

本政策**不预设所有 Logo 文件都自动被 MIT 许可排除**，也不会在未经核实的情况下声称商标已经注册或相关权利完全归某一方所有。在增加针对素材的独立限制或声明权利归属之前，维护者应核实素材来源、贡献者及现有授权，再明确记录适用的例外或单独许可。

## 5. 咨询与疑似冒充

如不确定某种使用方式是否会造成误导，应链接官方仓库并明确披露产品的运营方。如需报告疑似冒充官方或容易造成混淆的使用，请在 [DownVid 仓库](https://github.com/Downvid/downvid/issues) 提交 Issue，附上公开 URL 和简要说明。请勿发布用户的私人数据。
